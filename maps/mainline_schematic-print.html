NOTES

Capacities printed on the schematic are the average annual capacities of the Enbridge Mainline System Configuration, Q1 2026, as compiled in the house apportionment record; the configuration itself notes that its capacities do not include current restrictions. Where the configuration publishes only a combined or segment figure (Lines 3, 6, 14/64, 78), the schematic prints that basis and no invented per-line number.

Line 2A and Line 2B are sequential segments of one light stream (Edmonton to Clearbrook to Superior): their matching 442 kb/d figures are the same capacity stated for each segment, not additive parallel capacity. The same applies to Lines 6A/6B and the Line 14/64 pair.

The tasking note for this sheet expected CER No. 500-series international joint tolls for an Edmonton to Chicago path. The house tolls record carries no such current rows: the current filed record is CER No. 600 Canadian local tolls (effective 2026-07-01) plus the CER No. 5 / FERC No. 45.48.0 international joint tariff to Lockport (effective 2026-07-02), and those actual rows are what the sheet prints.

This page is a configuration schematic, a labelled format separate from the geographic maps in this atlas: stations are placed in configuration order and line runs show connections, not geography or distance. Nothing on this sheet should be read as a route map.

Line 9 is drawn as a boundary stub at Sarnia only. Line 9 is a separate system (tolls.db pipeline 11) with its own atlas entry and print sheet; duplicating its configuration here would drift from that record.

Line 10 is drawn and labelled as a former Mainline branch: it was sold to Westover Express Pipeline Limited (United Refining) under CER leave granted May 2019, the sale completed June 2020, and Westover Express has operated it since September 2023. It remains on the configuration because Westover is where Lines 7, 10 and 11 meet, and a schematic without it misstates the Ontario configuration.

Flanagan South (Line 59) and the Seaway connection south of Flanagan and Cushing are outside this schematic's line list: the sheet ends the southbound configuration at Flanagan and at the Line 55 (Spearhead) Cushing terminus. The Flanagan South international joint tolls in tolls.db (CER No. 6 / FERC No. 3.59.0, Edmonton to the Texas Gulf Coast) are therefore not printed here.

WITHHELD

Heavy Canadian local tolls under CER No. 600 are not printed: tolls.db carries the CER No. 600 light, medium and condensate rows as effective (19 rows) but no effective heavy row for the Canadian local paths; the only heavy Edmonton to Gretna rows in the record are superseded (Interim CER No. 529, CAD 4.7837/bbl, effective 2023-07-01, and the 2011 CTS-era tariff). Per the standing tolls rule, a toll missing from tolls.db is an ingest backlog item, never a market gap, and no superseded figure is printed as current.

Separate per-line capacities for Lines 6A and 6B are withheld: the Q1 2026 configuration publishes one Line 6 capacity (667 kb/d, Superior to Sarnia) and no segment split, so no segment number is drawn.

Separate per-line capacities for Lines 14 and 64 are withheld: the configuration publishes one combined Line 14/64 capacity (343 kb/d, Superior to Griffith/Hartsdale) and no split between the two legs.

A single Line 3 capacity is withheld: the record publishes segment capacities only (390 kb/d Edmonton to Hardisty as Line 3A; 760 kb/d Hardisty to Superior as Line 93). Printing either figure as the Edmonton to Superior Line 3 capacity would misstate the other segment.

A single Line 78 capacity is withheld: the configuration publishes segment capacities (570 kb/d Line 78A; 500 kb/d Line 78B) and no single Flanagan to Sarnia figure.

A Line 10 capacity is withheld: Line 10 left the Enbridge system in the 2019-2020 sale to Westover Express and carries no capacity in the Q1 2026 Enbridge configuration or the CER Mainline profile compiled here. The approximately 70,000 b/d delivered to Warren in United Refining's own description is a delivery volume, not a nameplate, and is not printed as one.

Current apportionment percentages are withheld by design: apportionment is a monthly, per-line and per-key-point outcome (CER profile), not a durable configuration number. The sheet states the basis and prints no live percentage.

Per-line throughputs and flows are withheld: the CER publishes Mainline throughput and capacity at key points, not as a per-line series for most segments, so no flow figure is attached to any line on this schematic.

No committed-service tolls are printed because none exist on this system: all Mainline capacity is uncommitted service under the Mainline Tolling Settlement (CER profile). The absence is the fact, stated here rather than left silent.
